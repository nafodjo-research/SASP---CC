\section{Econometric Details}
\label{app:details}

\subsection{Match-Level Model}
\label{app:match_model}

The match-level probability is specified as:
\begin{equation}
p_{itm} = \Lambda(\beta' X_{ijm} + \gamma' Z^{pre}_{ie} + \delta D_{ie} + \rho v_{ie})
\end{equation}
where $X_{ijm}$ contains pairwise covariates (ranking points difference / 1000 linear and quadratic, Elo difference / 100 linear and quadratic, surface Elo difference / 100 linear and quadratic, Laplace-smoothed H2H win proportion, H2H encounter count, age difference, and surface indicators) and $Z^{pre}_{ie}$ contains pre-treatment focal player characteristics (ranking points / 1000 linear and quadratic, Elo / 100 linear and quadratic, surface Elo / 100 linear and quadratic, and player age). The trajectory likelihood factorizes into match-level Bernoulli contributions, so estimation reduces to a standard logit on stacked match-level data.

\subsection{Control Function}
\label{app:first_stage}

The generalized residual correcting for endogenous LL assignment at non-Grand-Slam events is:
\begin{equation}
\hat{v}_{ie} = D_{ie} \cdot \frac{\phi(\Phi^{-1}(P_i^{LL}))}{P_i^{LL}} - (1 - D_{ie}) \cdot \frac{\phi(\Phi^{-1}(P_i^{LL}))}{1 - P_i^{LL}}
\end{equation}
where $\phi(\cdot)$ and $\Phi(\cdot)$ are the standard normal PDF and CDF. Including $\hat{v}_{ie}$ in the outcome equation corrects for selection on unobservables.

\subsection{Selection Probability Construction}
\label{app:instrument}

The selection probability $P_i^{LL}$ is computed from the full combinatorial structure of qualifying match pairings. A weighted logit of match outcomes is estimated on qualifying round matches at non-Grand-Slam LL-granting events, separately for each tour. Given predicted match-winning probabilities for the $N_e - 1$ other final-round qualifying matches, $P_i^{LL}$ is computed by exact enumeration over all $2^{N_e - 1}$ outcome combinations. When $N_e - 1 > 20$, Monte Carlo simulation with 10,000 draws approximates the probability. For the first-LL-restricted sample, the win model is re-estimated on the restricted event set (ATP: $N = 9{,}425$ qualifying matches; WTA: $N = 10{,}009$), and the selection probability is recomputed for the LL-naive pool at each event. The old-vs-new $P_i^{LL}$ correlation is 0.979, confirming stability.

\subsection{Inference for the Match-Level Model}
\label{app:inference}

Standard errors for the match-level model are cluster-robust at the player level, allowing arbitrary within-player dependence across matches and events. Fisher randomization inference for the ranking-point outcomes (Section~\ref{sec:results}) is reported alongside the clustered SEs and is preferred for the small Grand Slam samples ($N = 120$ ATP; $N = 82$ WTA).
