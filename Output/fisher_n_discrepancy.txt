The Fisher test uses complete cases for each outcome, dropping
observations with missing values at any horizon. Players who exit
the sample before 52 weeks have missing values at longer horizons.
This reduces effective N relative to the total skeleton count.
The N reported in Fisher tables reflects complete-case counts
divided by 5 horizons.
